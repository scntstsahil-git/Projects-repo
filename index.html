Countless known and unknown Indians made sacrifices for the dream of a free India.

Journey to Freedom

1857 Revolt of 1857 — a major uprising against British rule.
1885 Indian National Congress was founded.
1919 Jallianwala Bagh tragedy shocked the nation.
1930 Dandi March became a major symbol of resistance.
1942 Quit India Movement demanded an end to British rule.
1947 India achieved independence on 15 August.
